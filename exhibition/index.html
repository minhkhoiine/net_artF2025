To love, to recall: Intimacy in net art

Curated by Khoi Vu

Love has been a topic people always try to find an answer for the question of our heart. Let's find out different types of love through my exhibition

This online exhibition explores how love is expressed, shared, and transformed through digital net art. In digital spaces, affection takes on new forms, through hyperlinks, participation, and code, revealing how technology mediates emotion and connection.

Olia Lialina's My Boyfriend Came Back from the War presents a fragmented digital narrative of reunion, reflecting the distance and longing that shape modern relationships. Michael Samyn's Love reduces affection to its purest gestures: a smile, a name, a simple act of care, emphasizing how love can exist in both simplicity and silence. Kristen Cabrera's "Are you in love?" shows the user's initial name and their crush, with the crush saying that their love will last forever.

Together, these works explore love as both personal and communal, intimate yet shared, fleeting yet enduring. They remind us that in the digital world, love survives not only through connection but through the acts of remembering, creating, and feeling together. Moreover, love exists in every aspect of life.
