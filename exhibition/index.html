Net Art Exhibition

Curated by Khoi Vu

Love has always been a question at the bottom of our heart, something we countinually seek to express understanding and feeling. This exhibition presents various ways to expressing love

This online exhibition explores how love is expressed, shared, and transformed through digital net art. In digital spaces, affection takes on new forms, through hyperlinks, participation, and code, revealing how technology mediates emotion and connection.

Olia Lialina's My Boyfriend Came Back from the War presents a fragmented digital narrative of reunion, reflecting the distance and longing that shape modern relationships. Michael Samyn's Love reduces affection to its purest gestures: a smile, a name, a simple act of care, emphasizing how love can exist in both simplicity and silence. Kristen Cabrera's "Are you in love?" transforms digital interaction into a public confession, allowing users to put their initials beside their crush’s.

Together, these works explore love as both personal and communal, intimate yet shared, fleeting yet enduring. They remind us that in the digital world, love survives not only through connection but through the acts of remembering, creating, and feeling together. Ultimately, these works suggest that love exists both the physical and digital dimensions of our lives

Love includes many aspects of life

Love is created by Michael Samyn in 1995. Love presented in a pink background. In this work, the artist wanted to show that love has variety of ways. Mostly, he mentioned love in daily life such as seeing a landscape, or just a smile. By emphasizing LOVE's grid of lyrical and expressive vignettes, he also highlighted digital form has enabled new possibilities for expression and experimentation.

This piece of net art attracted me first by its name, LOVE. The more I explored, the more I knew that love is not anything complicated. It is as simple as enjoying everyday, seeing someone smile, or even writing the name of our love ones.

About the chosen net arts:

1. "My boyfriend came back from the war," created by Olia Lialina in 1996. Her project is about a young woman reunited with her boyfriend after he returns from war.

2. "Love" was created by Michael Samyn in 1995. Love is presented in a pink background. In this work, the artist wanted to show that love takes many forms.

3. "Are you in love?", created by Kristen Cabrera in 2025. It is a chance for users to use internet as a place to confess the hidden love the the one deserved.
